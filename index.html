Materials

AquaMarine Deck marine foam

We're an authorized dealer and installer of AquaMarine Deck, a closed-cell marine foam decking with a peel-and-stick backing. It's soft underfoot, grippy when wet, and custom cut to fit your boat.

  • 7mm thickStandard, with 5mm and 20mm options
  • 3M™ adhesiveMarine-grade peel-and-stick backing
  • Closed-cellWon't absorb water
  • Built for the sunUV, stain, mold & mildew resistant
